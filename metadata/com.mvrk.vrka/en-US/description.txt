VRKA Android brings the media extraction and browser fallback architecture of VRKA to mobile devices. Built natively with Jetpack Compose, Kotlin Coroutines, and local processing, it delivers direct media extraction powered by yt-dlp and FFmpeg, combined with an isolated Mozilla GeckoView browser fallback engine with integrated uBlock Origin content filtering and Puemos stream inspection.

Features:
* Direct Extraction & Download: Powered by yt-dlp and FFmpeg (arm64-v8a)
* Format, resolution (Best, 4K, 1440p, 1080p, 720p) and codec selection
* Audio extraction (MP3, Opus with native stream copy remuxing, 16-bit PCM WAV)
* Custom HTTP Headers with RFC 7230 token validation and CRLF protection
* Sensitive token and cookie redaction across logs and storage
* Passive Browser Fallback via Mozilla GeckoView
* Content filtering with integrated uBlock Origin
* Passive stream discovery with Puemos WebExtension
* Local on-device diagnostics without third-party tracking or telemetry
* Cryptographically authenticated component updates with OpenPGP signatures
