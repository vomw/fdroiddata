Notes Tech is an Android app for encrypted Markdown notes with per-folder
vaults, semantic search, and on-device AI — 100% local, zero Internet
permission.

'''Radical privacy''':
* INTERNET permission removed from the manifest (tools:node="remove" on 7
  transitive permissions) — the app is technically incapable of sending
  data. Verifiable via aapt dump permissions.
* No tracker, no telemetry, no third-party crash reporter.

'''Per-folder vaults''':
* Passphrase mode: Argon2id (m=64 MiB, t=3) + AES-256-GCM + AAD bound to
  folder_id for KEK wrap and to note_id for content.
* PIN mode: Keystore-bound (StrongBox/TEE) + lightweight Argon2id +
  auto-wipe after 5 failed attempts.
* HMAC verifier in constant time.

'''Panic mode''' (deterministic ordered sequence):
1. Force FLAG_SECURE
2. Cancel voice dictation
3. Lock all open vaults (zeroize folder_kek RAM)
4. Wipe Keystore PIN keys
5. Destroy SQLCipher KEK (point of no return)
6. Wipe DB header (16 MiB)
7. Uninstall AI models
8. Clear prefs and tmp

'''On-device AI (user-imported models)''':
* Q&A Gemma 3 1B int4 (Google, ~554 MB) — semantic RAG on top-K
* Whisper dictation (ggerganov/whisper.cpp, MIT)
* MiniLM-L6-v2 semantic search (bundled, MIT, ~22 MB)

'''DB''' SQLCipher AES-256 with Keystore-bound KEK CSPRNG 32 bytes.

'''Apache 2.0''' open source — full code on GitHub.
