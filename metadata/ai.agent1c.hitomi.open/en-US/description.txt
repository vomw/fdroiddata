Hitomi is a tiny floating hedgehog companion for Android, by Agent1c.ai.

Hitomi can:

* Float above your screen as a draggable hedgehog assistant
* Chat in a compact speech bubble UI
* Always-listening speech-to-text mode (optional)
* Open and use a mini "Hitomi Browser" window for browsing tasks
* Run optional advanced Linux-like commands through Termux (superuser mode)
