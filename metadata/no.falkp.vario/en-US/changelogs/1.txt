First release on F-Droid.

* Three altitude sources: GPS, terrain map (MAP) and the phone barometer (BAR).
* Native 10 Hz barometer readout, so BAR mode works in a native build where no
  browser exposes the pressure sensor.
* Classic 3-pointer altimeter with a Kollsman window, feet/metres toggle,
  tap-to-zoom dials and a debug overlay with pressure strip chart.
* No Google Play Services, no analytics, no ads. AGPL-3.0-or-later.
