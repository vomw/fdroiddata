Motivational App is a tiny ironic joke app built around one very specific idea:
type your name, tap the button, and receive a short burst of extremely direct motivation.

It is intentionally minimal. No account, no feed, no subscriptions, and no productivity theater.
Just one fast interaction, one ridiculous payoff, and a deliberately rude sense of humor.

Features:

- one quick name-based interaction
- English and Italian localization
- lightweight About menu
- small, fast, and intentionally absurd design

This app is not pretending to be a life coach.
It knows exactly how silly it is, and that is the point.
