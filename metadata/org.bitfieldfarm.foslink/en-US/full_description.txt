fOSlink is a mobile client for farmOS, the open-source farm management platform.

Connects directly to your own farmOS instance using PKCE OAuth2 - no intermediate
server or third-party service required. Your data goes straight between your phone
and your farm.

Features:

* Direct connection to any farmOS instance
* Secure login via PKCE OAuth2
* Activity logging - register work sessions with asset, category and flags
* Observation logging - record field observations
* Maintenance logging - track maintenance work on equipment
* Grain module - seeding and fertilization logs
* Todo - cross-module view of all pending logs
* Module system - enable/disable modules per device

Built with SvelteKit and Capacitor. No Google Play Services or Firebase.
